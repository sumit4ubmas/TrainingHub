Hardware 101: the ecosystem

Know every piece — what it does, its specs, and how to sell it

⏱ 8 min
All Plum hardware is built with commercial-grade equipment — heat-tested, spill-resistant, designed for the real chaos of a busy restaurant kitchen. This is not consumer off-the-shelf technology.

Core hardware — the four pillars

Core
POS Terminal
15.6-inch HD touchscreen, Android-powered, IP64 spill-resistant front panel. Central hub for orders, payments, menu management, and reporting. Speed-tested with zero lag during rush hours. If your staff can use a smartphone, they can master Plum in 10 minutes.
Core
Handheld POS
Mobile device for tableside ordering and payments. Accepts NFC, chip, and contactless payments at the table — staff never walk back to the counter. Keeps servers moving quickly, increases table turnover, and reduces errors from shouted orders. Fully integrated with the main terminal in real time.
Core
Self-Ordering Kiosk
Guest-facing self-ordering station that reduces wait times and upsells automatically on every transaction — consistently, without relying on staff. Lowers labor costs while increasing revenue per ticket. Ideal for QSRs, food courts, and high-traffic counters. All kiosk orders sync instantly with the POS.
Core
Kitchen Display System (KDS)
Replaces paper tickets with digital screens for kitchen staff — 100% order accuracy. No lost tickets, no misread handwriting, no wasted food. Works hand-in-hand with Order Right™ technology to ensure every order reaches the kitchen exactly as the guest intended.

Peripherals — available today

Peripheral
Plum Receipt Printer
High-speed thermal printers for customer receipts and kitchen chits. Fully integrated with the terminal — prints automatically on transaction completion. No separate configuration needed.
Peripheral
Cash Drawer
Secure cash storage, fully integrated with the Plum Terminal. Opens automatically on cash transactions. Eliminates the need for separate drawer management or reconciliation workarounds.
Peripheral
💳
PAX DEVICE
Credit Card Reader — PAX
PAX payment terminals integrated with Plum POS for fast, secure card acceptance. Supports NFC/contactless (Apple Pay, Google Pay), EMV chip, and magnetic swipe. PCI-compliant, tamper-resistant hardware. Works seamlessly at the counter, tableside, and at the kiosk for a consistent payment experience across the restaurant.
Peripheral
🖨️
KITCHEN PRINTER
Kitchen Printer
Dedicated high-speed thermal printer for the kitchen — prints order tickets automatically when orders are submitted from the terminal, handheld, kiosk, or delivery platforms. Ideal for kitchens that prefer printed chits alongside or instead of the KDS. Waterproof and grease-resistant paper support. Fully integrated — no manual routing required.
Peripheral
📊
BARCODE SCANNER
Barcode Scanner
Used for retail items, grab-and-go products, or quick inventory lookups. Fully integrated with the Plum Terminal — scan items directly into the order without manual entry. Ideal for restaurants with packaged goods or retail merchandise.

Refund mode — fast, accurate, protected

Plum's refund system captures the original transaction context — including all discounts and specialty pricing — so every refund is accurate and the business is protected.
1
Referenced refund
Locate the original order by transaction ID. Select which items to refund. Plum automatically applies any discounts or specialty pricing from the original check — ensuring accurate refunds, not full-price refunds on discounted items.
2
Unreferenced refund
For situations where the original transaction isn't available — process a standalone refund with manager authorization. Fast, simple, auditable.
Without referenced refunds, staff could accidentally refund a full-price item that was originally sold at a discount — costing the restaurant money on every return.

With Plum, he changes it once and it syncs to every screen and every platform in 10 seconds. He gets two hours back every single week — that's over 100 hours a year."

Tagging — Plum's most unique admin feature

Tagging is the only feature of its kind in any POS system. It lets you operate outside of normal predefined data structures — grouping anything together so you can make bulk changes with a single button press.
You can add tags to almost anything: menu items, locations, discounts, promotions, modifiers. This allows fast, thoughtful edits without having to touch every item individually.
Example 1 — Regional coupon deployment
You want to run a coupon for only 3 states. Tag the stores:
Southern California Northern California Texas
Apply the coupon to those tags → It activates at every tagged location simultaneously. One action.
Example 2 — Ingredient-based discount
You want to apply 10% off every dish that contains chicken:
#chicken
Tag all chicken items once → Apply the discount rule to that tag → Every chicken dish gets 10% off automatically. No manually updating 40 menu items.
Tagging allows you to assign or edit items across multiple stores with a single button press. No other POS system has this feature.
